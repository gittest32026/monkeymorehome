[Monkeymore Studio](https://www.monkeymore.app) has developed many products over the past few years. They are all classic games or tools, but with their own unique touches in the details.

## Mini Games

### Puzzle Games

- [Tangram](https://tangram.monkeymore.app) - Thousands of free tangram puzzles, from classic animal shapes to a vast problem library. Play puzzles online, or export them as PDF for kids.

- [Klotski](https://klotski.monkeymore.app) - 480 classic levels, from Heng Dao Li Ma to nightmare difficulty, sorted by minimum moves, with hints and auto-demo support.

- [Math 24](https://game24.monkeymore.app) - Hundreds of carefully designed levels: use addition, subtraction, multiplication and division to make 24 from four numbers.

- [Matchstick Equations](https://mathstick.monkeymore.app) - Move one or several matchsticks to turn an incorrect equation into a correct one. 400 levels from easy to hard.

- [Sudoku](https://sudoku.monkeymore.app) - Classic 9×9 Sudoku with 6 difficulty levels, plus variants such as Killer, Parity, Jigsaw and Consecutive. Supports puzzle generation, solving and photo recognition.

- [Rubik's Snake](https://rubiksnake.monkeymore.app) - An online interactive 3D Rubik's Snake: experience the classic Rubik's Snake puzzle.


### Casual Games

- [Jump Jump](https://jump.monkeymore.app) - A free 3D jumping mini game: click and play right in your browser.

- [Sokoban](https://sokoban.monkeymore.app) - Hundreds of levels: beat the campaign mode and browse community level collections.

- [2048](https://2048.monkeymore.app) - The classic 2048 number-merging game.

- [Number Games Collection](https://numberhunter.monkeymore.app) - Three number games that train focus and reaction speed: Number Hunter, Number Pop and Number Spot.

- [Coloring](https://colorfy.monkeymore.app) - Hundreds of free online coloring pages with beautiful SVG designs, a digital coloring game for kids and adults.

- [Jigsaw Puzzle](https://jigsaw.monkeymore.app) - An online jigsaw puzzle paradise: challenge your observation skills.

- [Tetris](http://tetris.monkeymore.app) - 40 Lines, Blitz, Zen, custom modes, player-vs-AI and multiplayer modes.

- [Spot the Difference](http://spotdiff.monkeymore.app/) - Daily levels and themed collections: a find-the-difference game that tests your observation skills.

- [Mahjong Solitaire](https://mahsolitaire.monkeymore.app/) - Six free online match games: Mahjong Solitaire, Mahjong Connect, Mahjong Crush, Tile Match 3D and more. No registration, no ads.

- [Solitaire](https://solitaire.monkeymore.app/) - Klondike, FreeCell, Spider, Pyramid and many other card games. No download or registration required.


### Board & Card Games

- [Chess](https://chess.monkeymore.app) - Interactive chess problem training: Chess Tactics Art (2434 problems), Polgar Checkmates (4457 problems), plus PGN import practice.

- [Chinese Chess](https://xiangqi.monkeymore.app) - Play online: create a room and invite friends, or challenge the AI engine.

- [Go](https://goboard.monkeymore.app) - 200 carefully selected classic life-and-death problems, covering beginner to advanced levels, with daily online practice.

- [Gomoku](https://gomoku.monkeymore.app) - Online gomoku matches.

- [Mahjong](https://mahjong.monkeymore.app) - A Japanese mahjong practice site: play with friends in private rooms, practice against bots, and a 300-question "what to discard" problem set.

- [Dou Dizhu](https://doudizhu.monkeymore.app) - A card game hall with seven poker variants: Dou Dizhu, Guan Dan, Shuang Kou, Pao De Kuai and more. Real-time multiplayer, with bots automatically filling in when there aren't enough players.

- [Billiards](https://billiards.monkeymore.app/) - Browser billiards with a realistic physics engine: solo classic 15-ball, creative levels, and real-time matches in snooker, Chinese 8-ball, American 8-ball and nine-ball.

### io Games

- [Snake](https://snake.monkeymore.app) - Multiplayer online snake: classic survival mode, 40-player online battles and custom skins.

## Tools

- [Image Tools](https://imageconv.monkeymore.app/) - Image format conversion (50+ formats), compression (WebP/AVIF), cropping, rotating and flipping. No login required, 100% private.

- [Video Tools](http://videotool.monkeymore.app/) - Video conversion, compression, cropping, merging, audio extraction, subtitling and more — all processed locally in your browser.

- [PDF Tools](https://pdf.monkeymore.app/) - Merge, split, compress, convert, rotate, unlock and watermark PDFs. All processing done locally in your browser, no registration required.

- [eBook Tools](https://texttool.monkeymore.app/) - eBook tools for MOBI/EPUB. All processing is done locally in your browser; files are never uploaded to any server.

- [LAN File Sharing](https://airdrop.monkeymore.app/) - AirDrop-like file transfer over the local network: direct end-to-end connections with no server uploads, real-time chat and large-file chunking with resume support. No registration required.

- [QR Code Generator](https://qrcode.monkeymore.app/) - Free QR code generator supporting square, circle and animated styles, with batch generation.

- [Avatar Generator](http://avatar.monkeymore.app/) - One-click custom avatars in 11 styles (DiceBear, Multiavatar, Robohash and more). High-resolution PNG with transparent backgrounds, no registration required.
